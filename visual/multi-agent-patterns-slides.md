### Multi-Agent Systems: The Future of AI Collaboration

---

## What Are Multi-Agent Systems?

- Systems where multiple specialized AI agents work together
- Each agent has specific capabilities and roles
- Structured communication and coordination
- Combined intelligence greater than individual parts

---

## Why Use Design Patterns?

- Proven solutions to common architectural challenges
- Clearer system organization
- Easier maintenance and scaling
- Better alignment with problem types

---

## Today's Patterns

1. **Route Pattern**: ONE-TO-ONE SELECTION
2. **Coordinate Pattern**: SEQUENTIAL PROCESSING
3. **Collaborate Pattern**: PARALLEL WORK ON SAME PROBLEM
4. **Loop Pattern**: ITERATIVE IMPROVEMENT CYCLES
5. **Network Pattern**: ANY-TO-ANY COMMUNICATION
6. **Hierarchical Pattern**: TOP-DOWN AUTHORITY

## Pattern Comparison

|Pattern|Communication|Processing|Decision Authority|
|-------|-------------|----------|------------------|
|Route|Centralized|Directed|Router selects|
|Coordinate|Linear|Sequential|Stage-specific|
|Collaborate|Mesh|Parallel|Consensus|
|Loop|Circular|Iterative|Evaluation-based|
|Network|Any-to-any|Dynamic|Distributed|
|Hierarchical|Top-down|Layered|Level-specific|

---

## Pattern Selection Guide

1. Clear sequence of specialized steps? → **Coordinate**
2. Need to select one specialist for each task? → **Route**
3. Iterative improvement the primary goal? → **Loop**
4. Clear hierarchy of authority needed? → **Hierarchical**
5. Flexible direct communication needed? → **Network**
6. Multiple perspectives on same problem? → **Collaborate**
